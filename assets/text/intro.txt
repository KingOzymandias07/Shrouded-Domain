Dammit. You think, as you twist your keys in the ignition. You hear the engine rev but it doesn't completely start up.
You twist the key several more times, to no avail.

You open your car door and step out onto the street. There is a chill in the air. There are no other cars around.
You try to look around, but the fog here is so dense, you can only see a couple of yards in front of you.
As you squint your eyes, you can barely make out a sign that says, "All Saint's Chapel".

Maybe someone in there can help me.

You shiver. Damn its cold. You quickly walk toward the chapel.
You can hear the organ playing as you walk up the stone steps. The giant wooden doors of the chapel are heavy and old.
They creak as you open them.

The organ sounds sorrowful as you enter the chapel. It plays low tones that remind you of heavy rain.
You look around. You see no one in the pews. But there must be someone playing the organ.
You spot the organist playing their instrument from the back of the chapel.

Even though the music is low and sad, the organist seems almost gleeful. Their movements are light and semi-robotic.
They have a stretched smile on their face.

You hesitantly walk toward the back of the chapel.
The organist is sitting in an the organ box on a raised platform. Making you have to look up to speak with them.

"Um, hey, sorry to bother. But my car broke down and I could use a little help."

The organist didn't stop playing. They didn't even look up from the keys.

"Um. Hello?"

You wave your hand around to try and get their attention.
The platform is made of old wood. You don't really trust it to hold all of your weight.

You look down at your feet as you step up to the organist.

"Hello?"

You look down again as you go to the opposite side of the platform, where the opening to the organ box is.
You look up.

"HOLY SHIT!" you scream as you jump back.

Half of the organist face is covered in pulsing purple fungus.

The plant is blooming from their cheek and forehead, and is bleeding from their eye socket.

The same purple fungus even covers their neck and shoulder.

"Nope. Nope. Nope. Nope-" you whisper to yourself as you sprint back to the front door.

You sprint back to your car, tearing open the passenger door.
You fiddle with your keyring to find the glove box key and unlock it.

A revolver sits there. No ammunition.

You slam the car door shut, as you look behind you, another grotesque amalgamation peers at you through the fog.
At least you think it does. You think its best to avoid the fog for a bit.
You look up at All Saint's and shudder.

"What are these things?!"

You stumble away from your car as the fog seems to creep forward with every second spent there.

"Shit, I've got to get out of this fog."

You ran to the only place not covered in a dense fog.
