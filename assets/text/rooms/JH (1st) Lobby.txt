You shiver. The nostalgia turns sour. This place is a physical embodiment of an unpleasant memory. 
You are now standing an odd vestibule-like area. The brown stained doormat under your feet is frayed at the edges. There is a large cork board on the wall, with a variety of event posters thumb-tacked on it. The posters have been there for a while- all of the events listed have already passed. 
Even with the doormat and posters, this room feels empty. No one has been here in some time.
