This hallway is longer than it should be. 
The walls look like they were once white. Now, they are a moldy gray color. It looks like there is some water damage on the ceiling that has creeped into the drywall. Other than the stains, the walls are completely bare. The bright white lights hurt your eyes, making you blink a couple of times. There are a couple of doors on either side of the hall. 
The stillness of the hall is unsettling.
