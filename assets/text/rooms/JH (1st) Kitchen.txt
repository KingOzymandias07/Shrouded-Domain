An intense rotting smell pierces you. You hold your nose under your sleeve. 
The countertop is coated in a thin layer of dust. 
There are old dirty dishes in the sink. How long have these been here? 
There is an old refrigerator in the corner. It is slightly ajar, and isn't running. There is a tipped-over half gallon of milk, the cap is gone and some of the milk has leaked out. The expiration date says it expired weeks ago. 
Your eyes begin to water from the stench.
