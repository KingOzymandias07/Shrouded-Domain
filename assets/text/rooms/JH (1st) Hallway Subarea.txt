This room is comparatively darker than the other hallway. 
It relies on the adjacent hallway's lights to keep it illuminated. Making this
small room feel suffocating and private. There small black steps with rickety old
railings that lead to this area. There is a small window on the far wall, dark
green algae grows on it. There is a single landscape painting hanging in the middle
of the left wall. You would much rather be in that painted landscape, instead of
the dreadful place you are currently standing in. 
If you died in here, your body probably wouldn't be found for a while.
